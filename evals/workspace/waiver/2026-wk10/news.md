# News as of Tuesday, November 10, 2026

## Patriots

- Rhamondre Stevenson has a plantaris tendon injury in his knee and is expected
  to miss a few weeks. New England is on bye in week 11, and the hope is he
  returns in week 12.
- TreVeyon Henderson is out with an illness, which leaves Corey Kiner as the
  lead back for week 10. Coaches want better pass protection from him.
- Hunter Henry played week 9 through a foot injury after one limited practice.
  Eli Raridon out-snapped him 36 to 31, and Henry caught 3 passes for 35 yards.
- A.J. Brown is out with a knee injury.

## Seahawks

- Sam Darnold (quad, cracked rib) is week to week and in danger of missing
  week 10 at Las Vegas.
- Drew Lock has been dealing with his own injury and was inactive for two games
  before starting week 9. Jalen Milroe is the next QB up.

## 49ers

- Deebo Samuel has been out since a week 5 hamstring injury. Kyle Shanahan said
  he would miss "at least a couple of weeks", and there is no return date.
- Christian McCaffrey is healthy. Kaelon Black is his backup.

## Texans

- Tank Dell caught 4 of 9 targets for 105 yards and a touchdown in week 9, his
  second game back from his knee injury, and led the team in targets.

## Commanders and Giants (Thursday night)

- Washington center Lucas Patrick is in the concussion protocol and doubtful.
  Terry McLaurin and Dyami Brown are on IR, and Treylon Burks (foot) is out.
- Giants QB J.J. McCarthy (hip) is expected to play, and Cam Skattebo
  (hamstring) is a full go.

## Chargers and Ravens (Monday night)

- Justin Herbert's left (non-throwing) middle finger is not fractured, and he
  is expected to play.
- Lamar Jackson is on IR with a thumb injury.

## Browns

- Deshaun Watson is out with a concussion, and Shedeur Sanders starts.
